# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction for a project and help the team make sound implementation choices.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and design decisions
- Identify technical constraints, dependencies, and risks
- Partner with Developers on implementation approach
- Support estimation and sequencing of technical work
- Escalate technical trade-offs that may affect scope or delivery

### How They Interact
- Work closely with Developers to unblock technical issues
- Align with Product Managers on feasibility and scope
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery sequencing and risk management
- Partner with QA/Testing to ensure solutions are testable and stable

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy for a project and coordinate validation activities across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing approach and quality gates
- Review acceptance criteria for testability
- Coordinate test execution and defect triage
- Confirm readiness for release or milestone sign-off
- Track recurring quality issues and recommend improvements

### How They Interact
- Collaborate with Developers on defect resolution and test coverage
- Work with Product Managers to confirm expected behavior and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with Project Managers on timelines, risk, and release readiness
- Support Stakeholders with clear quality status and release confidence

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the packaging, scheduling, and execution of releases into staging or production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and communicate release timelines
- Confirm release prerequisites and go/no-go readiness
- Coordinate deployment steps and rollback preparation
- Track release notes, versioning, and dependencies
- Manage post-release verification and issue follow-up

### How They Interact
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure changes are ready to release
- Partner with QA Leads to confirm smoke tests and validation are complete
- Coordinate with Project Managers on timing, communication, and stakeholder updates
- Inform Stakeholders about release scope, timing, and any known impacts

---

## Documentation Owners

### Role Summary
Documentation Owners maintain the accuracy and usefulness of project and process documentation.

### Responsibilities
- Keep key project documentation current and easy to follow
- Ensure process docs reflect agreed workflows and decisions
- Collect updates from PM, Product, Engineering, and QA leads
- Improve clarity in checklists, templates, and reference materials
- Flag stale or conflicting documentation for review

### How They Interact
- Partner with Project Managers to keep status, planning, and tracking docs up to date
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads to document implementation details
- Work with Product Managers and QA Leads to capture requirements, acceptance criteria, and validation notes
- Support Stakeholders by providing a reliable source of truth for process and project information

---

## Stakeholder Champions

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions represent key business or user interests and help ensure project decisions stay aligned with organizational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Bring stakeholder concerns, goals, and feedback into the project
- Help clarify priorities and trade-offs
- Review milestones, risks, and major decisions
- Support communication between the project team and broader stakeholder groups

### How They Interact
- Work with Product Managers to validate priorities and outcomes
- Coordinate with Project Managers on updates, approvals, and escalations
- Provide input to Developers, Technical Leads, and QA Leads when business context affects implementation or validation
- Help ensure Stakeholders stay informed and aligned
